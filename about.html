ABOUT APOLLO FORGE & FITTING

ENGINEERED FOR PRECISION.
BUILT FOR INDUSTRY.

Apollo Forge & Fitting is a trusted manufacturer, supplier, and exporter of high-precision forged fittings, industrial flanges, butt weld fittings, and specialized piping products engineered for critical industrial applications across global markets.

WHO WE ARE

ENGINEERING STRENGTH.
INDUSTRIAL CONFIDENCE.

Apollo Forge & Fitting operates as a dedicated industrial manufacturer and global exporter, delivering heavy-duty forged fittings, butt weld components, and specialized piping products built to withstand severe operating environments.

With a steadfast commitment to precision machining, dimensional repeatability, and metallurgical integrity, our products serve demanding installations across oil & gas refineries, petrochemical complexes, thermal power stations, and offshore infrastructure.

Backed by comprehensive raw material sourcing, tight quality control, and extensive stockholding, we partner with EPC contractors, industrial fabricators, and engineering enterprises worldwide to fulfill standard and bespoke industrial piping requirements.
