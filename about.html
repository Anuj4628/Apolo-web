CORE INDUSTRIAL EXPERTISE

CLASS 3000# / 6000# / 9000#
HIGH-PRESSURE PIPING

FORGED FITTINGS

High-integrity Socket Weld and Screwed-Threaded (NPT / BSPT) forged fittings engineered for severe industrial service, extreme hydraulic pressures, and vibration-intensive installations.

  • Manufactured in strict adherence to ASME / ANSI B16.11, BS 3799, and MSS-SP specifications.
  • Comprehensive range: 90° & 45° Elbows, Equal & Reducing Tees, Couplings, Unions, Caps, Hex Nipples, and Weldolets.
  • Available in Carbon Steel (ASTM A105 / A350 LF2), Stainless Steel (304/316/317L), Duplex, and Alloy grades.
PIPELINE CONNECTIONS

INDUSTRIAL FLANGES

Precision-machined forged steel flanges designed for rigid, leak-proof mechanical joints in mission-critical oil & gas, chemical processing, and power generation pipelines.

  • Full standard coverage: ASME / ANSI B16.5, B16.47 Series A & B, DIN, BS 4504, and EN 1092-1.
  • Pressure ratings from Class 150# to 2500# (PN6 to PN400) in sizes from 1/2" to 48" NB.
  • Configurations: Weld Neck (WNRF), Slip-On (SORF), Blind (BLRF), Socket Weld, Threaded, and Spectacle Blinds.
STANDARD ASME B16.5 / B16.47
WALL SCH 10 TO SCH XXS
FLOW INTEGRITY

BUTT WELD FITTINGS

Seamless and welded butt weld components engineered for uniform wall thickness, smooth hydraulic flow transitions, and superior resistance to cyclic thermal expansion.

  • Manufactured in full conformity with ASME / ANSI B16.9, ASME B16.28, and MSS-SP-43 standards.
  • Components include Long & Short Radius 90°/45° Elbows, Equal & Reducing Tees, Reducers, Caps, and Stub Ends.
  • Complete schedule coverage from SCH 10 to XXS with 100% radiographic inspection and dimensional certification.
